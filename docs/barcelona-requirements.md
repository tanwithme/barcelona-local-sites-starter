# The minimum useful Barcelona business website

**A visitor should understand the offer, trust the facts, and take one useful next step in Catalan, Spanish or English.** This is the starter's product standard. It does not mean every business needs the same design, or that every item below is a universal legal obligation.

Use this checklist during drafting and review. The owner confirms business facts and permissions; the builder confirms actual website behavior. Legal guidance below is bounded by its cited source, checked on **7 September 2026**. Have a qualified local adviser resolve the relevant business and sector obligations before publishing legal text, taking payments or adding regulated features.

## 1. Collect one set of facts

| Visitor's question | Minimum owner-approved content | What blocks release |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What is this place? | Public business name, business type, actual offer and neighbourhood. One plain headline and short explanation. | Invented owner history, awards, credentials, customer counts, guarantees or unsupported “best in Barcelona” claims. |
| Is it for me? | A short list of services/menu categories, what each includes, relevant restrictions and who it suits. | Attractive labels with no concrete explanation; invented suitability or accessibility claims. |
| What will it cost? | Owner-approved consumer price, unit/session/duration, relevant supplements; or a clear explanation that a quote is required and how cost is determined. | Example prices, unexplained “from” prices, unclear tax status or contradiction with the real menu/provider. |
| Where can I go? | Correct street name, number, postal code, municipality and map destination. For mobile services: accurate service area and whether the address accepts visits. | Invented storefront, wrong map pin, conflating the Barcelona municipality with the metropolitan area. |
| When can I use it? | All seven days, closed days, split shifts, kitchen/service hours if different, temporary closures and holiday exceptions confirmed by owner. Use Europe/Madrid for time calculations. | Missing Sunday, presenting a midday closure as continuous service, calculating “open now” without exception data. |
| How do I act? | One primary action; phone/directions fallback; what happens next and when to expect a reply if the owner can commit to it. | A WhatsApp link that promises a confirmed booking, a dead provider URL or an unanswered channel. |
| Can I trust it? | A few rights-cleared photos, truthful owner/work details, an approved external review-profile link if available. | Invented testimonials, unlicensed social photos, artificial images passed off as real premises/results. |
| What if I need help? | Visible contact route, essential FAQ, applicable legal/privacy information and service restrictions. | A customer journey that ends at a chatbot, untranslated critical terms, or legal placeholders. |

Store shared facts once and localize the reader-facing copy. When a price or hour changes, compare all three languages and the business's booking/menu/profile sources. Record who approved it and when. The same facts should appear in visible copy and structured data.

## 2. Three complete language journeys

The project requirement is **Català · Español · English**. Show language names as text, not flags. Preserve the reader's current page when switching. Provide direct, shareable language URLs and a readable default page; avoid forcing a different language through location detection.

Use Spanish appropriate to Spain, natural Catalan for the local business and clear international English. Keep official street names and brand spelling. Barcelona detail comes from actual neighbourhood, transport/access and service facts—not generic Gaudí, beach or “hidden gem” language.

The following are starter microcopy examples, **not certified native-language or legal translations**. Have fluent local speakers review tone, meaning and line breaks before client publication.

| Intent | Español | Català | English |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp enquiry | Consultar por WhatsApp | Fer una consulta per WhatsApp | Ask on WhatsApp |
| Ask about an appointment | Consultar disponibilidad | Consultar disponibilitat | Ask about availability |
| Enquiry is not confirmation | La cita solo queda confirmada cuando el negocio te la confirme. | La cita només queda confirmada quan el negoci te la confirmi. | Your appointment is confirmed only when the business explicitly confirms it. |
| Directions | Cómo llegar | Com arribar-hi | Get directions |
| Call | Llamar | Trucar | Call |
| Restaurant menu | Ver la carta | Veure la carta | View the menu |
| Price list | Servicios y precios | Serveis i preus | Services and prices |
| Price tax note, only if true | IVA incluido | IVA inclòs | VAT included |
| Price requires assessment | Pide presupuesto | Demana pressupost | Request a quote |
| Check closure exceptions | Consulta los horarios de festivos. | Consulta l'horari dels festius. | Check public-holiday hours. |

Review the full journey: page title, navigation, headings, service descriptions, price qualifiers, opening hours, alt text, button labels, FAQs, legal notices, error/empty states and the destination or prefilled message of the primary action. Do not translate only navigation and call the site trilingual. Do not promise that the business can speak all three languages unless the owner confirms it.

**Catalan legal scope:** Generalitat business guidance identifies commercial information, restaurant menus, quotes/contracts/invoices, fixed signs and information necessary to use goods/services among the material to supply at least in Catalan. Its short business guide explicitly includes advertising of products/services **with prices** on websites and social media. This is a reason to include Catalan in the core content workflow; it is not a claim here that every sentence of every website universally requires translation. Confirm the specific business's obligations with the owner/adviser. [Agència Catalana del Consum: business obligations](https://consum.gencat.cat/ca/empreses/requisits-obligatoris/obligacions-generals-per-a-les-empreses/), [Generalitat: business language guide](https://llengua.gencat.cat/ca/serveis/empresa_/orientacions/breu-guia-regentar-negoci-empresa-a-catalunya-normativa-lingueistica/).

## 3. Choose the lightest useful action

| Business | Sensible first action | Information to include before the action | Add complexity only when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Restaurant / bar | View menu, request a table, or link to the existing reservation provider | Cuisine, consumer prices, address, kitchen hours, group policy and confirmed dietary/allergen information route | The owner already operates reservations and can explain availability, confirmation, cancellation and data handling. |
| Barber / beauty | Ask availability or link to the existing appointment provider | Service, price, duration, walk-in policy, location and opening days | The owner needs real availability and commits to keeping the booking system current. |
| Café / bakery | Directions or call | Location, hours, offer and any genuine ordering/pickup policy | Preorders or retail sales justify stock, payment, fulfilment and support work. |
| Repair / mobile trade | Call or request a quote | Service area, availability limits, assessment/call-out charges, qualifications only if verified | Dispatch or scheduling has a real operational owner. Do not invent 24/7 or emergency guarantees. |
| Class / studio | Ask about a first session | Level, language of instruction, price, duration, materials, venue and attendance requirements | Capacity, recurring payments or a class calendar can be reliably maintained. |

Prefer an ordinary WhatsApp link over a chat widget for the initial enquiry route. Use only the business's confirmed number. Any prefilled text should be generic; do not put visitor names, health details, private messages or tracking identifiers in URLs. Opening a chat does not send a message or reserve capacity. Verify the exact destination on a real phone with the owner, stopping before sending unless they approve the test. Keep a visible phone number and a useful fallback for people without WhatsApp.

An enquiry site has fewer data-handling components, but it is **not automatically free of personal-data processing**. Hosting logs, external resources and the messaging provider still need consideration. Before launch, inspect the actual platform/site, including network requests, storage and external embeds; write privacy information that matches what it does. Data minimization is the operating principle. [AEPD: data protection by default](https://www.aepd.es/guias/guia-proteccion-datos-por-defecto.pdf).

## 4. Price, legal information and privacy

- **Consumer prices:** show the total including applicable taxes and explain supplements or the method for determining a quote. Avoid a bare “+ IVA” consumer headline. The general consumer law addresses total-price information; Catalan restaurant guidance specifically rejects an “IVA not included” menu note and requires clarity about terrace/table/bar differences or supplements. Have the owner confirm the correct tax treatment; the builder must not invent it. [Consumer law, Article 60](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-20555#a60), [Consum: bars and restaurants](https://consum.gencat.cat/ca/detalls/article/Bars-i-restaurants-00003).
- **Legal identity:** gather the actual operator's applicable legal identity, contact and registration/professional details for review. Spain's LSSI Article 10 requires specified provider information to be permanently, easily, directly and freely accessible electronically. A brand name alone is not a completed legal notice. Do not publish fake IDs or expose a personal home address as a convenience; resolve the lawful business-contact details. [LSSI, Article 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758#a10).
- **Cookies and embeds:** begin with no analytics, ad pixels, embedded maps, social feeds or remote chat widget. Use normal links and locally hosted permitted assets. This is a design choice, not proof that a hosting platform sets no cookies. Audit first. If consent is needed, prevent non-exempt storage until consent and make rejecting as straightforward and prominent as accepting; support withdrawal. Do not add a decorative banner that fails to control anything. [AEPD cookie guide, May 2024 edition, sections 2–3](https://www.aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies.pdf).
- **Forms, newsletters, payments or accounts:** treat each as a new scope decision. Identify who processes data, purpose/legal basis, minimum fields, retention, providers and applicable notices before implementation. A checkbox does not make a system compliant. Never paste customers' private submissions into public AI tools or a public repository.

## 5. Instagram is a source lead, not a photo license

Ask the owner for original files and written permission for website use, including permitted crops/edits and any required attribution. Record the asset, rights holder, permission evidence, allowed use and any expiry. A business can possess a photographer's photo without having unlimited reuse rights. Public accessibility and giving credit do not by themselves create a license. Spanish intellectual-property law reserves relevant reproduction/public-communication rights, including protection for photographs. [Intellectual-property law, Articles 17 and 128](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-8930).

Separately check permission or another valid basis for identifiable people. Permission from the venue is not automatically permission from a customer pictured there; exercise particular care around children. Choose empty-premises, object or owner-approved work photographs when possible. AEPD explains that identifiable images are personal data and that publication needs a legitimate basis. [AEPD: removing photographs and videos](https://www.aepd.es/areas-de-actuacion/internet-y-redes-sociales/eliminar-fotos-y-videos-de-internet).

If rights are uncertain, keep the source link in private research and use a clearly marked placeholder in a private draft. Do not hotlink, scrape/repost or embed a social feed as a way around rights or privacy review. A reference site's photography is not part of this starter's asset license.

## 6. Local search without invented authority

Use unique translated page titles and descriptions, a single clear main heading, readable service descriptions and genuine locality. Keep name, address/service area, phone and hours consistent with the owner's public business information. Do not create fake neighbourhood pages, fake branches, review stars or unearned certifications.

For translated pages, use separate URLs such as `/ca/`, `/es/` and `/en/`, correct document language, and reciprocal `hreflang` links including each page itself. Use full absolute live URLs in those links. `ca`, `es` and `en` are valid language choices; a page for Catalan speakers does not need a fake country code. An `x-default` can point to a deliberate fallback. Google recommends explicitly indicating alternate language versions and warns against forced language redirects that prevent access. [Google: localized versions](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/localized-versions), [Google: multilingual sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/international/managing-multi-regional-sites).

Set a self-canonical for each intended language page, use the real production domain, and publish only canonical, available URLs in the sitemap. Keep private drafts out of indexing; `noindex` alone is not access protection. Add suitable `LocalBusiness` structured data only from approved visible facts. Validate it after deployment; do not add ratings merely to pursue stars. Structured data does not guarantee search features or ranking. [Google: LocalBusiness](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/local-business).

## 7. Quality requirements for the finished site

The starter's target is WCAG 2.2 AA-informed implementation, followed by testing. Use semantic headings and landmarks, readable text rather than menu screenshots, meaningful image alternatives, labelled controls, keyboard access and visible focus. Check reflow/zoom, reduced motion and that overlays never hide the main action. Standard text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast; large text has a 3:1 threshold. Prefer generous 44 × 44 CSS-pixel controls; WCAG 2.2 AA's target-size criterion specifies 24 × 24 with exceptions, so 44 is the starter's stronger design choice, not a misquoted AA minimum. [W3C WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/).

Legal accessibility applicability depends on the service and business. Spain's Law 11/2023 includes ecommerce services and contains a defined service-microenterprise exemption. Do not claim every small brochure site automatically falls under identical obligations, or that small size removes every accessibility duty. Check applicable national, Catalan and sector requirements; keep the accessible design baseline either way. [Law 11/2023, Articles 2–3 and Annex VII definitions](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2023-11022).

Before calling the site ready, check:

- Mobile and desktop layouts, all languages, long service names and accents; no clipped prices or horizontal scrolling.
- Main action, phone and directions destinations; provider unavailable; WhatsApp unavailable; missing images; slow network; JavaScript disabled where a static route is promised.
- Static hours remain visible even if an “open now” enhancement fails. Never silently infer holiday openings.
- No secrets, private briefs, customer data, fake legal identifiers or demonstration prices in the deployed files or public repository.
- Real owner review of facts/assets, fluent-speaker copy review and a real phone test. Automated checks alone do not establish accessibility, native copy, customer understanding or legal compliance.
- Ownership, domain/hosting renewal costs, who edits, backup/export, rollback and a supported contact are documented.

## 8. Finding clients in Barcelona

Start with warm introductions or a short in-person conversation at a suitable moment. Ask for permission to send a specific, useful preview. Record the requested channel and scope. The message should explain one observed problem and a bounded offer; do not send shame-based audits, invent lost revenue or imply you already work for them.

Do not turn public emails or WhatsApp numbers into permission for promotional messages. LSSI Article 21 generally prohibits unsolicited/unapproved promotional email and equivalent electronic communications, with a qualified existing-customer exception. AEPD explicitly discusses SMS and WhatsApp in its unwanted-advertising guidance. One-to-one and business-to-business are not automatic exemptions. Use permission-based follow-up; get qualified advice before designing broader outreach. [LSSI, Article 21](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758#a21), [AEPD: unwanted advertising](https://www.aepd.es/areas-de-actuacion/publicidad-no-deseada).

Keep outreach and research separate. A public `tanwithme` provenance marker may identify voluntarily credited sites; it must not become visitor surveillance. Explain it to the site owner, keep it removable, and do not interpret a marker as proof of ownership, customer demographics, native-language quality or business results.

## 9. Make the next project easier

After an owner-reviewed handoff, record one failure, its cause and the smallest useful rule change. Promote a new feature only when observed use justifies its maintenance. A successful first rep is a site the owner understands and a visitor can use—not a large number of prompts or a complex stack.
