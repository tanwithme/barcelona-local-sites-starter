# What changed in the design method

This update abstracts an owner-requested review of a private client-design conversation and its adopted creative brief. Raw conversation, client assets, identifying personnel details and private feedback are not included. These are interpretations of specific corrections, not a universal profile of the author's mind or measured conversion results.

| Observed correction in the source work | Inferred reusable principle | Exception / disconfirming check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Request for intentional QA and removal of habitual ornament | Judge the reason an element exists; remove empty decoration | Meaningful link affordances and language punctuation remain |
| Request for personality and coherence after cleanup | Keep shared grammar while allowing section differences | A complex working tool may need stronger repetition |
| Editorial direction shifted configuration later in the journey | Sequence around the visitor's motivation and readiness | Urgent visitors need direct access immediately |
| Later feedback said the polished revision lost playfulness | Preserve the emotional seed through revision; polish can erase identity | Ask whether the audience values that feeling in this context |
| Request for belonging, limited color roles and a distinctive shape language | Build a small world from relationships, materials and gestures | Do not make every industry playful or give every surface a blob |
| Scarce photographs prompted a request for original illustrations | Change the visual mechanism while preserving truth and feeling | Illustration cannot substitute for evidence of real work |
| Correction of maker prominence and redundant identity labeling | Hierarchy expresses authorship; honor confirmed roles and remove duplication | Do not invent an origin story or alter a real business name |

## Repository audit and resulting changes

The earlier pack already had bounded setup, isolated clients, translation/fact contracts, static fallbacks and release checks. Its weakness was that “distinctive design” was largely an instruction without a method: fixed sector colors, a near-identical scaffold, generic shape advice, no explicit protection against creative regression, and Make prompts that prescribed the default palette.

The revised pack routes the existing six skills through one [designer playbook](designer-playbook.md), embeds that method directly into generated Make and Sites handoffs, adds worked contrasting directions, and separates functional acceptance from creative review. The portable examples now vary composition and hierarchy as well as color. They remain fictional, text-led practice sites, not finished client brands or proof that every generator can execute the method.

## The controlling constraint

A novice should not need expert taste to know the next useful move. The copilot must demonstrate a choice, explain its purpose, inspect the actual result and preserve what worked. More prompts alone do not solve that problem. We retain six skills and eight task prompts, with deeper references loaded when relevant.

## Where this can fail

The agent can still imitate the examples, overuse organic shapes, or declare an attractive draft successful without visitor evidence. Use the [design review](../templates/design-review.md) to catch those failures. Preserve the source snapshot; test a small change; roll it back if the protected quality is lost. Native-language acceptance, client satisfaction and enquiry outcomes require real people and are not established by this repository update.
