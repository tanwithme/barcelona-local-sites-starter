# Barcelona website references

Start with a visitor's decision: **Is this the right place, can I use it, and what do I do next?** A restaurant, a barber and a repair service share that job. Their answers should look and sound different.

These are eight selected learning references, **not a ranking of Barcelona's best websites or an endorsement of the businesses**. Selection favors useful visitor tasks, different business models and one instructive failure. Prices and operating claims on the source sites are not independently confirmed.

## What was actually inspected

On **7 September 2026**, public page text, headings, visible link labels and destinations returned by web retrieval were inspected at the URLs below. Bar del Pla's three language pages were read separately. Two sites also received the bounded rendered inspection described below. This does not establish full mobile usability, accessibility conformance, performance, consent behavior, image ownership, successful booking, or business results. No forms were submitted, bookings made, consent controls used, or businesses contacted. Some text retrievals can be cached; the date is the inspection date.

## Eight references, eight useful lessons

| Business / inspected surface | Observed on the source page | Principle to borrow | Boundary and improvement to test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bar del Pla, restaurant, El Born** — [Català home](https://www.bardelpla.cat/), [Español](https://www.bardelpla.cat/es/), [English](https://www.bardelpla.cat/en/) | Language links; menu and wine-menu downloads; street/map, phone, hours and a recognizable food-and-wine voice across the three pages. | Give each language a complete route to the same practical answers. Place location/contact near the introduction. Let specific food and people create character. | Borrow the information order, not its sentences, menu or images. These pages also illustrate why translation needs editing and why every weekday must be explicit. Test an HTML menu before relying on a download. |
| **Bar Cañete, restaurant, El Raval** — [home](https://barcanete.com/) | Repeated reservation links, food/menu links, family history, address/map, phone and legal links. | Let one main action recur after the visitor has enough information. Use a short, concrete owner story to explain the offer. | The external reservation flow was not completed. A booking provider is an added dependency and may carry costs/data processing. Do not copy the family story, food claims, photography or branding. |
| **NOMAD Coffee, several locations** — [contact / locations](https://nomadcoffee.es/pages/contact) | Each named location has its own address, map destination and hours. | Store hours and contact details per location. Make the nearest usable location easy to find. | A small first client rarely needs a store locator, shop, newsletter or subscription system. Borrow the location clarity only; no assets or brand treatment are licensed here. |
| **Three Marks Coffee, café and retail** — [main home](https://www.threemarkscoffee.com/) | Product prices, subscription/shop routes, café directions, academy route and a location labelled as opening in the future. | Clearly separate visiting a café, buying beans and attending a class. Show an unavailable location as unavailable. | Retail and subscriptions expand payments, fulfilment and support scope. Do not add them to a café brochure automatically. Do not treat source prices or future-opening status as current client facts. No photos or product descriptions may be reused without rights. |
| **Barber Sant Joan, Eixample** — [Spanish home](https://www.barbersantjoan.com/es/) | Named services and prices, Booksy links, opening days including Sunday closure, phone, WhatsApp, map/review links and gallery references. | A barber needs a legible service list, a way to request or book an appointment, and a fallback contact. Explain walk-ins only if the owner confirms the policy. | Reviews are the site's displayed claims, not independently checked here. Do not copy reviews, customer faces, prices or gallery files. Keep legal/consent interfaces in the chosen language too. |
| **La Torratxa, ceramics school, Gràcia** — [home](https://www.latorratxa.com/) | ES/CA links, a beginner-friendly description, recurring classes versus one-off workshops, session times, price, calendar, reserve/contact links. | Explain who can join, what happens, what is included and how the schedule works. A beginner should not need to understand the craft to choose a first session. | Capacity, exact dates, inclusions and cancellation rules need a maintained source. Link labels/destinations deserve checking: one contact label in the retrieved page pointed to WhatsApp. Do not copy workshop imagery or descriptions. |
| **Lampistería Barcelona, repair service** — [contact](https://lampisteriabarcelona.es/contacto/) | Phone and WhatsApp, stated office hours, service area, a distinction between office and walk-in premises, and out-of-hours availability described as case-by-case. | For a mobile service, explain where it works, how contact is handled and what happens after an enquiry. A management address is not necessarily a storefront. | The site's speed, pricing and review claims are unverified. Do not promise emergency availability or fixed photo-based quotes without owner evidence. If customers send photos, tell them to avoid unrelated people, documents and personal details. No copying of photos or claims. |
| **Solina's Barber Shop, Les Corts — counterexample** — [home](https://www.solinasbarbershop.com/) | Services/prices, neighbourhood, booking/directions links and gallery labels. The retrieved public page explicitly says its listed prices are examples requiring confirmation before publication; its dynamic opening-status text was unresolved in retrieval. | Public-looking polish does not prove content is ready. Build a release check for demonstration prices and essential information that depends on JavaScript. | **Do not reuse these prices or treat this as a launch-ready model.** The inspected domain presents itself as this business; owner control was not independently established. No visual or functional verdict is claimed. Copy neither assets nor branding. |

## Bounded visual inspection: two real first impressions

The [Bar del Pla Catalan home](https://www.bardelpla.cat/) and [Barber Sant Joan Spanish home](https://www.barbersantjoan.com/es/) were opened in an isolated browser session on **7 September 2026, 12:58–12:59 UTC**. Their initial visible viewports were captured and visually read at **390 × 844** and **1440 × 1000 CSS pixels**. These are browser viewport checks, not physical-phone tests. Four screenshots and browser snapshots remain outside the repository; the businesses' imagery is not redistributed with this pack.

| Reference | What the rendered views showed | Design lesson for the starter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bar del Pla** | Desktop: navy brush-style lettering, yellow accents and menu buttons over a large interior photograph. Phone: the large name wraps, while a cookie panel covers the lower hero/menu area. Language choices remain at the top. | Let real premises and a restrained palette establish character. Test text against every background; use a simpler body face for practical information. |
| **Barber Sant Joan** | Desktop: a prominent logo and yellow-outlined booking button over an interior photograph. Phone: compact logo/menu button, visible booking action, small overlaid introduction and a large cookie panel. The Spanish page's consent interface mixes English and Catalan. | Keep the main action recognizable as the layout changes. Localize the interface around the page too; test small text over photographs. |

Both sites loaded. Their initial cookie panels occupied substantial phone-screen space; nothing was accepted, rejected, dismissed or bypassed. Consequently, this check covers the visible initial state only—not the post-consent page, menus, carousels, scrolling journey or booking flow. Background photographs differed between Bar del Pla captures; this was not a controlled comparison of the same image. Contrast ratios, loading speed and consent compliance were not measured. The recommendations above are design judgments from these visible states, not measured conversion claims.

## What this changes in the starter

The repeated useful structure is **offer → useful details → proof → action → practical information**. It is a starting hypothesis from these inspected surfaces, not measured conversion evidence. A café may need directions most; a repair service may need a phone call; a barber may already use an appointment provider. Ask the owner which customer action they can actually support.

Keep a stable foundation: readable type, consistent spacing, one recognizable primary action, truthful facts, accessible controls, translated journeys and portable content. Change the composition and visual character using the business's own materials: its sign, interiors, craft, dishes, staff voice and neighbourhood context. A universal system should preserve quality while allowing difference.

For the first paid project, recommend **one location, a small service/menu list and contact or directions**. Make the agreed Barcelona experience available in Catalan, Spanish and English without creating three independent fact sheets. A simple static site can handle that; a database, login, newsletter, live Instagram feed, chat assistant and checkout each need a separate reason to exist.

ChatGPT Sites must remain a conditional route. Do not assume that a Barcelona-based beginner is eligible because they have ChatGPT. Check current account, plan and regional availability against the [official Sites guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339-creating-and-managing-chatgpt-sites) before promising that route. The portable brief and local build should remain usable when it is unavailable.

## The smallest learning experiment

Choose one consenting local owner and one fictional or owner-approved draft. Give three readers the same tasks: one reads Catalan, one Spanish, one English. Ask each to find the offer/price, determine when and where they can visit, and start the intended action without sending a message or making a booking. Record confusion and wrong expectations in plain words. Fix the first repeated problem. Do not describe three readers as a representative Barcelona audience.

Ask the owner to update one real item—such as a closing day or a service price—using the handoff. If they cannot do it, reduce the editing burden or agree an explicit maintenance service before expanding features.

## Research limits and excluded leads

- No owner/customer interviews, paid analytics, continuous page recording, native-speaker acceptance or sales outcomes were available for this audit.
- Barcelona Barber Shop initially returned page content, then timed out on repeated requests; it is not used as a core reference here. The Trono and Noï Taller failed direct retrieval in this pass. Search snippets from them were treated as leads only.
- A separate `webnueva` Three Marks domain appeared in search. The principal domain above was used instead of treating a secondary/staging-looking surface as the canonical source.
- This selection is concentrated in food, grooming, craft classes and repairs. It does not establish requirements for healthcare, legal services, finance, accommodation, regulated trades certification, marketplaces or ecommerce.
- Source-site photography, logos, customer reviews and copy remain with their rights holders. Links are research references, not a reusable asset library.
