# Design direction

Date: 2026-09-07. This is a reusable local-business starting point, not a claim of a finished client brand.

**Job:** help someone on a phone decide whether a nearby business fits and take one clear next step. A beginner should be able to change the content without a new application stack.

**First-principles choice:** the controlling constraint is asking a novice to make expert decisions before they have one successful example. Delete the mandatory subscription, blank-page design interview, customer database and automatic publishing. Deliver one complete trilingual static example, then add integrations only when observed use requires them.

**Language:** Catalan, Spanish and English are separate complete pages backed by one shared fact set. Names, prices, numbers and hours are not three independent sources of truth. Preserve Barcelona place names and accents. Local fluent review is required for a real business; generated localisation is not proof of a native speaker's approval.

**Concept:** a clear neighbourhood shopfront with room for character. The tension is expressive typography with predictable actions. The strongest element is one large, specific customer-facing statement. Three sector grammars change the information order: restaurant = menu and visit; barber = services and appointment expectations; trades = problem and coverage.

**Composition:** small identity/language band; asymmetric introduction and useful facts; full-width service rows; contact; legal links. No fabricated review wall, decorative dashboard or repeated feature-card grid. Cards are allowed only for genuine peers.

**System:** shared semantic tokens for text, surface, action, border and focus. Restaurant uses a warm terracotta accent and serif display; barber uses bold sans type and navy; trades uses practical labels and deep green. System fonts, no remote font calls. The pack's own branding must not replace the client's identity.

**Responsive truth:** the main action remains near its explanation; columns become a reading sequence, not squeezed desktop coordinates. All essential information and links work without JavaScript. Real images are optional until rights are confirmed; the demo is intentionally text-led, not synthetic evidence of a real premises.

**Checks:** meaningful Node tests for missing translations, output escaping, release gates, marker verification, stale output and paths; responsive/manual keyboard checks where available; no claim of accessibility certification. Public business launch additionally needs owner approval, actual destinations, rights, local-language review, live privacy checks and handoff.

**Learning:** one observed failure, its smallest repair and a repeatable check. Promote a shared rule after two real projects by default; a serious documented failure can justify immediate protection, reviewed after the next use.


## Revision: 2026-09-11

The controlling design constraint was under-specified judgment: novices received a safe scaffold but too little help preserving character. [The playbook](designer-playbook.md) now protects the intended feeling, varies section rhythm within a common grammar, uses scarce assets creatively and learns from actual corrections. Existing palette choices are fallback examples. See the [audit](design-learning-audit.md) for the source-grounded reasoning and [worked examples](design-worlds.md) for contrasts. This revision does not claim owner acceptance or conversion gains.
