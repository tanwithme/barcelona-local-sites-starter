# Design a world that belongs to this business

This is a working method, not a house style. Use it to build the actual website. Explain only the decisions the learner needs now, in Catalan, Spanish or English. A beginner does not need to fill out a strategy workbook before receiving help.

## 1. Protect the reason someone cares

Name the visitor, their situation, what they should feel, and what they should be able to do. Separate these from the requested technique. “Feel welcome before asking about a gathering” is an experience; a configurator, animated plate or full-screen photograph is only one possible way to produce it. For an urgent repair enquiry, reassurance and quick access can matter more than an atmospheric first act. Never make customers complete a story before reaching help.

Read the current artifact and corrections before redesigning. Preserve the strongest line, real image, relationship or working interaction. Do not turn one rejected execution into a ban on its whole expressive family. A cleaner version can still be a creative regression.

## 2. Find the business, not its category stereotype

Use observed cues: how the owner works, what regulars value, real materials, a characteristic gesture, the setting, permitted work samples. Label inferences. A postcode does not establish identity; Barcelona does not imply tile motifs, a tourist palette or interchangeable neighbourhood copy. Keep the actual business name distinct from a changing offer and geographical context. Give makers credit in the order and roles the owner confirms.

Write a short private direction: intended feeling; protected seed; one governing visual idea; one practical customer action. Then consider three mechanisms briefly—perhaps photography, typography, or an illustrated material world—and choose one that fits available assets, time and maintenance. Do not present a mandatory selection menu or build three entire sites.

## 3. Coherence permits difference

Keep a small common grammar: type hierarchy, color roles, shape family, image treatment and recognizable actions. Vary scale, density, alignment, section backgrounds and pace according to each section's job. An opening can invite, a service list can explain, a proof moment can reassure, and a closing can start a conversation. They need not share one container.

Start with two dominant colors and one supporting accent when it helps focus the design. This is a useful constraint, not a universal law. Functional focus/error colors and readable contrast take precedence. Sector palettes supplied by the starter are fallback examples, not client identity; keep them only when they fit. If changing palette, update CSS and exported tokens together.

Define shapes as a family with roles: for example a wide serving-oval for an illustration, a shallow curved section edge for a welcome, and a simple pill for an action. Repeat the relationship, not the exact silhouette. Use a few deliberate contours, generous internal space and responsive variants. Random blobs, huge border radii on every card, tilted text and a different motif in every section do not create a world. Keep text and focus outlines outside decorative clipping; do not distort photographs or hit targets.

## 4. Personality must do a job

For each conspicuous choice ask: what does this help someone feel, understand or do here? Remove the elements with no answer. Avoid automatic stars, ornamental underlines, redundant eyebrows, numbered sections and dot-separated labels added from habit. None of those marks proves AI authorship. Preserve meaningful link underlines, keyboard focus, decimals and the Catalan middle dot in words such as col·laboració.

Play can help people imagine an occasion, discover an offer or compose an enquiry. Use it when it serves that purpose. Keep an immediate contact route and an understandable static fallback. Hover cannot be the only way to discover a service; reduced motion and touch users need the same content. A small meaningful response is better than requiring a game before contact.

## 5. Make scarce assets a creative constraint

Inspect the assets actually supplied. One truthful photograph with an intentional crop can carry more identity than six repeated placeholders. Show image purpose and mobile crop; keep faces, hands, work and food intact where their relationship matters. With no suitable photography, make typography or a coherent illustration family the main visual material.

Original or generated illustrations are allowed as illustration, never as evidence of real premises, staff, dishes served, finished work or customers. Write an art brief for the family: subject, silhouette vocabulary, stroke weight, color roles, perspective, texture, transparent background, intended placement and small-size legibility. Inspect every result and reject inconsistent extras. Use actual available generation tools only; do not pretend a filename is an asset. Do not reuse a reference brand's characters, artwork or identity. If generation fails, a deliberate text-led layout is a complete fallback.

The portable starter currently accepts approved local raster images with rights evidence. To ship SVG, inline icons, richer hero media or interactions, deliberately implement and review that extension; do not put unsupported data into the brief and expect it to render. Decorative images should not repeat nearby text to screen readers; extend the current image contract deliberately if it needs decorative roles.

## 6. Copy is part of the composition

Lead with a recognizable customer situation and a concrete offer. Keep verified service detail easy to find; emotional language must not hide what the business does. Let the action sound like the business while saying what happens next. A WhatsApp enquiry starts a conversation and remains editable; it is not a booked appointment, checkout or automatically sent message.

Preserve brand meaning across Catalan, Spanish and English, with one shared set of facts. Rewrite idiom, line breaks and labels naturally; do not translate word by word or shrink long translations until unreadable. Do not force a decorative uppercase treatment on all supporting copy. Test language switching and, if present, preserve selected options and enquiry state. Human fluent review remains separate from automated checks.

## 7. Build, look, revise

Build one complete private draft. Inspect the actual page at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px in all three languages; include full-page rhythm and close inspection of the opening, densest section and main action. Check images, contrast, keyboard focus, zoom/reflow, long labels, image absence and relevant empty/error states. Test the real enquiry destination without sending a message. If interactive, add/remove/reset choices and switch language; compare visible summary with the encoded message.

A passing build or screenshot without overflow is not evidence of good design. Ask separately: is there one recognizable idea? Does the page move between emphasis and rest? Are all sections competing? Is there a memorable detail grounded in the business? Can a new visitor find the offer, place and next action? If two businesses could swap names and remain convincing, investigate what identity is missing. This is a critique prompt, not a mechanical rejection of every reusable component.

If visual tools are unavailable, report visual review as not checked. Do not claim exceptional design from prompt wording alone. Keep the last working version and fix the observed weakness without discarding the successful parts.

## 8. Learn without fossilizing taste

Record locally: expected experience → observed behavior or specific owner correction → smallest change → retest → remaining uncertainty. Distinguish owner preference, model critique, automated checks and actual visitor behavior. Never invent a customer reaction or conversion improvement.

Start a new rule as a project-specific candidate. Try it in a different context before promoting it; retain an exception and expiry/review condition. A restaurant's need for appetite before configuration should not delay an urgent repair contact. Protect against serious factual, accessibility or data failures immediately. Preserve rollback and update only the affected guidance after a cheap private experiment. Do not automatically collect analytics, contact visitors, modify global memory or push a lesson into another client's files.

Teach one choice at a time: “I chose this because…; here is what to look for; change this one thing if you want to try.” Give more independence when requested or demonstrated. Help remains available at every stage.
