# A small design review

Keep completed client reviews in ignored `private/`. The copilot fills this from actual work, explaining only the next useful choice. Do not require a novice to complete it before helping.

- Visitor situation and intended feeling:
- Protected seed (line, relationship, asset or interaction):
- Source facts / inferred cues / missing evidence:
- Chosen concept and why it fits available resources:
- Shared grammar: type, color roles, shape family, imagery:
- Section jobs and where the pace changes:
- One conspicuous element removed and why:
- One meaningful detail retained or added and why:
- Fallback without photos / animation / optional tool:

Functional readiness and creative judgment are separate; neither certifies customer impact. Record unmet owner/fluent-language checks separately in the release record.

## One learning loop
