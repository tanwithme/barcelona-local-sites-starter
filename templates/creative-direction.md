# One business, one coherent world

The copilot fills this in the client's ignored `private/creative-direction.md` file, then implements and explains one useful choice. Keep the approved direction with that client and read it on the next design pass. It is not a mandatory interview.

- Visitor / situation / useful next action:
- Intended feeling and strongest existing idea to preserve:
- Real cues from the business; inference and unknowns:
- A productive tension (e.g. generous food / quiet interface):
- Page form and how it changes layout (editorial spread, service counter, workbench…):
- Main visual move; one strongest element per viewport:
- Two dominant color roles A/B, actual values and why they belong here:
- Third supporting color, specific job and where it must stay quiet:
- Character font and working font: names, roles, available licensed files/fallbacks:
- Type specimen: real headline, service row, price, longest CTA in ca/es/en:
- Shape family with roles; one deliberate variation:
- Section jobs, pacing, image crops and mobile order:
- Optional play/motion and equivalent static/touch/keyboard behavior:
- Generic default to escape and its smallest structural replacement:
- Three materially different mechanisms considered briefly; selected one and why:
- Source/CSS/token files changed together; current preview/version:
- Visual, functional and discovery checks actually performed:
- Owner preference vs observed visitor behavior vs model judgment:
- One next experiment, expected observation, exception and rollback:

A new builder needs this chosen direction included in its prompt. The generated pack carries the shared method; the copilot must add this client's actual approved direction rather than implying a generic export knows it. Keep private notes out of exports.
